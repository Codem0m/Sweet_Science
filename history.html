Who Is Spencer Swope?

Life and Career

I was born in Fremont, Ohio in 1963. At the age of 7, I saw my first boxing match which was for the unified Heavyweight Championship between Smokin' Joe Frazier and Jimmy Ellis. I immediately fell in love with the sport. As a kid, I would coordinate boxing matches in my back yard with my friends, using trees and string as a makeshift ring. As a teenager, I boxed on and off when I wasn't playing football. I went on to persue my childhood dream of boxing at the age of 28, after 10 years of living a life of hedonism. I spent a brief time in Vegas living and training with Doc Broudus, former trainer of two-time heavyweight champion, George Foreman. After returning to North Carolina, I fought professionally for the next 4 years.
