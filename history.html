About Spencer Swope

My Boxing Background

I was born in Fremont, Ohio in 1963. At the age of 7, I saw my first boxing match which was for the unified Heavyweight Championship between Smokin' Joe Frazier and Jimmy Ellis. I immediately fell in love with the sport!


As a kid, I would coordinate boxing matches in my back yard with my friends, using trees and string as a makeshift ring. As a teenager, I boxed on and off when I wasn't playing football.


I went on to persue my childhood dream of boxing at the age of 28, after 10 years of living a life of hedonism. I spent a brief time in Vegas living and training with Doc Broudus, former trainer of two-time heavyweight champion, George Foreman. After returning to North Carolina, I fought professionally for the next 4 years.

The Impact Boxing Has Had on My Life

Boxing has always been there for me when I was at my lowest points in life.
It helped me through some tough moments and gave me a feeling of self-worth.
Eventually it led me to a closer relationship with God, which I am eternally grateful for.

Mission

I want to help folks in their quest to get in better shape, lose weight, overcome stress/anxiety, while learning a great form of self-defense, as well as self-discipline.
